About MS

A project to introduce and bring to your table tasty, fresh, quality and highly addictive Nepalese momo. Momos are Favourite snack food.

Our momos🥟🥟🥟 combines some unique flavours particularly from the Eastern region where they originate. They are freshly made, juicy and bursting with flavour, made from the freshest local produce with a focus on quality ingredients following the highest standard of hygiene. We want to make our momos your best choice when it comes to comfort food!

Momo Trivia

When did Momo first appear in pakistan?

the Gilgit-Baltistan region of Pakistan has had its own traditional variation called mamtoo for generations, the commercial, street-style momos gained widespread popularity in major Pakistani cities only around the early 2020s, heavily popularized by localized dumpling brands.
